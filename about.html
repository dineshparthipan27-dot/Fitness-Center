Our Journey

THE EVOLUTION OF IRONFORGE

From a small garage gym to a world-class fitness destination. Here is how we forged our legacy over the years.

2015

THE HUMBLE BEGINNING

Started in a 500 sq.ft garage with basic free weights, a single squat rack, and a massive dream to change lives.

2018

EXPANDING HORIZONS

Moved to a commercial space, introduced advanced bio-mechanic machines, and launched our first group training programs.

2021

THE ELITE UPGRADE

Transformed into a premium facility. Added a dedicated CrossFit arena, VIP spa, and recruited industry-leading trainers.

2026

A GLOBAL COMMUNITY

Now hosting over 5,000 active members, IronForge stands as a symbol of uncompromised quality and relentless dedication.
