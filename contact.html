Knowledge Base

YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

Everything you need to know before stepping into the IronForge territory. Clear, direct, and straightforward.

Do you offer day passes or trial sessions?

Yes, we offer a one-time complimentary day pass for local residents. If you're visiting from out of town, day passes are available for $25. This gives you full access to the gym floor and recovery areas.

What is included in the Elite Membership?

The Elite Membership is our all-access pass. It includes 24/7 gym access, unlimited group batches, 4 personal training sessions per month, and unrestricted access to our spa, sauna, and cryotherapy recovery center.

Are personal trainers available 24/7?

While the gym floor is open 24/7 for our members, personal trainers are available on-site between 5:00 AM and 10:00 PM. However, you can pre-book special late-night or early-morning sessions directly with your assigned coach.

Can I freeze my membership if I travel?

Absolutely. We understand that life and business require travel. You can freeze your membership for a minimum of 14 days and up to 3 months per year at no additional cost. Just notify us 48 hours in advance.

Is there a parking facility available?

Yes, we provide an exclusive underground parking facility for our members. It is monitored 24/7 by our security team and includes dedicated spots for Elite members and EV charging stations.
